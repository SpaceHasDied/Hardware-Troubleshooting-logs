# Motherboard and PC Case Inspection

**System:** Custom Desktop
**Category:** Hardware Installation

## Problem
The system/case needed to be checked before installing the motherboard and other components.

## Diagnostic Process
1. Checked the motherboard mounting holes against the standoff locations in the case.
2. Inspected the motherboard for visible damage around the CPU socket, VRMs, and mounting points.
3. Checked the CPU cooler mounting hardware.
4. Verified the planned components were compatible with the power supply and cooling setup.
5. Dry-fitted the motherboard and cooler hardware before final assembly.

## Findings
Several case standoffs did not line up with the motherboard's mounting holes and needed to be removed.

## Resolution
1. Removed the extra, misaligned standoffs.
2. Confirmed the remaining standoffs matched the motherboard's mounting pattern.
3. Gathered the required tools and cleaning materials.
4. Completed a full dry fit before final motherboard installation.

## Result
The motherboard fit correctly in the case with no standoff interference, and the cooler mounting hardware lined up correctly.
